Kyoto, Japan

The Obsidian Pavilion

Explore Architecture south
Year 2024
Status Completed
Size 320 m²
Typology Private Cultural Center

A dialogue between shadow and silence.

The Obsidian Pavilion emerges from the dense forest of Kyoto as a monolithic intervention. Drawing inspiration from the traditional Japanese art of Shou Sugi Ban, the exterior is clad in heavily charred cedar, providing a resilient and light-absorbing facade that grounds the structure within its natural environment.

Internally, the project explores the interplay of volcanic glass and refined timber. Dark, polished obsidian surfaces reflect the surrounding moss gardens, blurring the boundary between the built form and the wild landscape. Large-scale structural glass panels allow the seasons to dictate the interior atmosphere, creating a sanctuary of shifting light and profound stillness.
